$$\int_0^\infty \frac{log(x)log(1-x^2)}{x^2} \, dx = \pi$$
$$4\int_0^\frac{\pi}{2} \frac{\log(\tan(x))}{1+\tan^2(x)} \, dx = \pi$$
$$\int_0^\frac{\pi}{2} \frac{\sin(x)+\cos(x)}{1-\sin(x)\cos(x)} \, dx = \pi$$
